recurrent state, episodic recall, learned plasticity, retrieval/write credit and
adaptive allocation. It does not establish our full held-out native-function claim
with ordinary-state resets and both forgetting/content controls. Reuse this search
before launching duplicate research; consult full text before porting equations.
