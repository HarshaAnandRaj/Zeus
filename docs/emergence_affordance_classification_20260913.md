# Engineered affordances and observed organization

User clarification2026-09-13: Zeus receives engineered affordances it can exploit;
we then distinguish what code guides from what develops within that setup.
Engineering is not itself a disqualification for emergence. Machines are physical
systems; the operational distinction here is prescription versus organization
arising through learning/interaction, rather than natural versus artificial.

This is a discovery/classification rule, not a new functional threshold, charter
amendment or automatic attribution of life, consciousness or usefulness.

For each concrete property, record the following separately:

| Question | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|
| What affordance did we supply? | Architecture, state/action interfaces, available observations, resets, storage limits, world rules and permitted learning routes. |
| What behavior did we explicitly prescribe? | Hardcoded decisions, masks, scheduled events, supervised labels, direct losses and evaluation targets. An evaluation target alone does not train a property. |
| What was observed? | Actual trajectories, denominators, parameter/source identities and precise magnitude, timing or strategy. Separate observer records from actor inputs. |
| What organization was left unspecified? | The measured strategy, interaction, setpoint or temporal/state pattern that is not fixed by those prescriptions. Degree can be unspecified even when some dependence is structurally guaranteed. |
| What alternatives remain? | Bugs, normalization, saturation, sampling, geometry, distribution shift, externally supplied triggers and other straightforward explanations. Surprise or conflict with intent is not sufficient proof. |
| What causal evidence exists? | Matched interventions/controls, intact replay, changed affordances or sources, repeated observations and the limits of each instrument. |
| Is it useful? | A separate functional diagnosis, including negative or neutral findings. Utility is not required to admit a discovery. |

Use explicit provisional labels rather than one umbrella verdict:

- **Engineered mechanism:** the property is fixed by code or a structural rule.
- **Explicitly taught behavior:** the measured behavior directly matches a supplied
  action/representation target. Its learned implementation can still contain
  unprescribed organization worth observing separately.
- **Observed unprescribed pattern:** a concrete unexpected organization is present;
  its mechanism/attribution remains unresolved. Keep it even when harmful or small.
- **Supported emergent organization:** evidence supports an organization that was
  not directly specified, while identifying the engineered conditions, causal
  support, alternative explanations and remaining limits. This does not buy a
  functional pillar or establish subjective experience.

Examples relevant to the current work:

- Protected eligible writes and declared fast resets are engineered. Useful
  agent-chosen inspection, selective retention and cross-cycle benefit are separate
  properties to test; persistence alone cannot supply all three.
- LMB5's proposed immediate feeding labels are explicitly taught. A novel useful
  information-gathering strategy, unexpected state encoding or stable interaction
  that arises inside the available interfaces could be investigated separately.
- LBT2's correct safe-patch arrival followed by starvation-producing WAIT preference
  at low-energy births is an observed unprescribed transfer pattern. The prior
  high-energy teacher legitimately taught WAIT in other states; neither the harmful
  transfer nor a specific attractor was prescribed or causally established.
- A route that forces information through state designs the existence of state
  dependence. Its measured magnitude or strategy may still be unspecified; the
  structural dependence and unforced degree must not be conflated.

Keep following small stones. Measure first, explain next, diagnose function
separately, and only then decide which mechanism or new experiment is justified.
