# Native body: observations worth following, including pathologies

Observation ledger, not a new functional gate or emergence verdict. We keep small
unexpected patterns before judging utility. LMB1 is closed VOID with independently
verified frozen-rule FAIL; LMB2's correction source/budget/endpoint are already
frozen. These observations cannot tune that ongoing campaign.

1. **Off-route WAIT preference.** Fatal LMB1 histories contain3,132 WAITs at interior
positions, and the intact policy argmax is WAIT on3,122 interior records. Public
demonstrations contain no interior WAIT/HARVEST labels. The sampled policy spends

An unprescribed learned behavior is observed; a dynamical attractor or its causal
mechanism has not been established. The empirical learner-state coverage contrast
is frozen separately as LMB2; no action mask hides the original phenomenon.

2. **Physiological readings can disagree about healthy operation.** All110 fatal
learned cases cross the energy death threshold;109 do so with integrity still
above threshold and one crosses both. None has integrity-only termination. Median
terminal integrity is.8647436, maximum1.0; minimum0 belongs to the simultaneous case.
The earlier diagnostic's single cause category gives energy precedence and must
not be interpreted as excluding simultaneous integrity failure. World recovery
rules are designed; high integrity beside starvation is a compositional failure,
not demonstrated intrinsic resilience or emergent self-maintenance.

3. **Memory content still controls the isolated first decision after motor fitting.**
All original native/delayed content regressions pass on the preserved LMB1 endpoint
in independent frozen calculations, despite motor qualification failing. Accurate
initial retrieval and reliable continuing operation are different abilities. This
is the central reason not to infer a whole memory pillar from a decoder or query.

## Offline causal question, frozen before extraction

On every learned interior-position record whose intact policy argmax is WAIT,
compare instantaneous distributions with fast state h retained/zero and slow state
z retained/zero in a2×2 table. Keep exact public observation, previous executed
action/reward/boundary, model parameters and outcome grouping fixed. No intervention
action executes and no recurrent/world trajectory is resimulated. First reproduce
the recorded intact distribution within2e-5. Public teacher labels are diagnostic
comparisons only. Include every parent, inherited/empty condition and surviving/
fatal outcome; do not pick a favourable case or model. Report all denominators.

This tests whether existing internal state influences the immediate off-route
preference under matched readings. State-zeroing can be off distribution and cannot
prove recovered viability, endogenous authorship, attraction or learned utility.
Any useful new question must receive a fresh protocol; observation class alone
does not buy a functional transition. Probe: training/probe_lmb1_interior_state.py.
