# OBS5: recorded preparation histories and slow/null interventions

The user authorizes the proposed real-history follow-up ("Let's go for it").
Freeze protocol and tested instrument before compute. Frozen CYC6 GRU memory
components only, not ZeusCore or the voice. Recorded experience is generated by
the supplied preparation procedure; "natural history" here means a state formed
by those actual observations rather than an arbitrary injected vector. It does
not imply self-selected experience, autonomous encoding goals or authorship.

## Scope and provenance

Use OBS2R's eight cached teacher streams (world offsets0,1,126,127; both
orientations) and eight initial/final weight pairs. Reconstruct hidden states
from each stream's first16 inputs from zero, in float64. The opposite orientation
is the donor history. Compare reconstructed states with the corresponding saved
initial_hidden (trained intact/swapped and own untrained with donor counterpart),
maximum absolute tolerance1e-5 for original float32 versus float64 arithmetic.
Keep both orientations; they are related cases, not independent samples.

For each model, preparation, and weight kind, take a pair hA,hB produced by the
actual two observation histories. All later inputs are identical within every
pair. Drivers: teacher stream[16:512] and 496 copies of stream[16]. Thus128
combinations per weight kind,256 total. No new world rollout or invented teacher
observations. There is no filtering by prior usefulness or survival.

## Fixed component selection

Let m=(hA+hB)/2 and delta=hA-hB. At m and the first common input compute J, its
frozen J^64 approximation, and readout-null basis N. Require rank9 for the9x32
readout. Set deltaN=NN^T delta. Let v be the unit slow-null direction selected
by the largest right singular direction of J^64 N (same rule as OBS4), and
u=v(v^T delta). No later output is used in selecting v.

A norm-matched null displacement control uses the alternating-sign unit vector,
projected into N and orthogonal to v, normalized and signed to have nonnegative
dot product with delta. If projection norm<=1e-12, use the first standard basis
vector whose projection exceeds that threshold. Let c=||u|| times this vector.
This is an equal-norm control displacement, not necessarily removal of a true
orthogonal component of delta; it can overshoot its directional projection.
Report it honestly as such. Save all vectors, norms and projection identities.

Construct six paired difference arms, all around the same midpoint:

* original: delta;
* remove_slow_null: delta-u;
* matched_null_shift: delta-c;
* remove_all_null: delta-deltaN;
* only_null: deltaN (remove the row-space difference);
* erase_difference: zero (identical starting states).

For scales1 and.5, use m plus/minus scale*arm_difference/2. Scale1 original is
exactly the reconstructed history pair, up to rounding; .5 and the other arms
are explicit counterfactual states. Do not clip. Replay496 updates. This is24
branches per combination,6144 total. Save actual states at0,1,4,16,64,128,256,496;
save whole hidden/logit/prediction separation curves and final signed vectors.
Check initially invisible only_null logits and identical erase arms within1e-10.
Also check that the three null-removal/shift arms preserve the original pair's
immediate logit difference within1e-10: their causal effects must arise later.

## Two separate diagnostic questions, fixed before exposure

A. NULL_HISTORY_ROUTE passes when ||deltaN||>=1e-8 and, at both scales, the
only_null arm's final hidden norm divided by its starting norm is>=.01, and
final sigmoid-output difference divided by that starting norm is>=1e-4.
This tests sufficiency of the projected history difference in counterfactual
states. It is not evidence of its necessity in the original full states.

B. SLOW_NULL_REDUCTION passes when, at both scales, original final prediction
separation>=scale*1e-5; removing u reduces that separation by>=25%; and its
fractional reduction exceeds the matched-null-shift reduction by>=.10.
Reduction is1 minus arm gap/original gap. Zero original gaps have no ratio and
fail this resolved-effect criterion. A pass is a selective reduction of a
measured history-dependent prediction difference, not proof of semantic
correctness, necessity of all memory, useful action or an authorship pillar.

Report both PASS/FAIL counts independently, continuous effects, all failures,
both drivers and weight kinds, and per-model denominators. Half-scale persistence
is a robustness diagnostic, not proof of linearity. These conventions are not
post-hoc rescue rules and are not functional survival bars.
