# LMT1: audited maintenance failure

The frozen long neural maintenance endpoint is FAIL. The independent audit
passes its evidence checks and confirms that none of 2,048 bodies survives the
4,096-step horizon. Source controllers are the earlier qualified LMB4 grounded
models, not newly fitted LMB5 controllers. No pillar is promoted.

Four parents operate on 128 fresh ecologies each, with inherited or empty memory
and enabled or disabled tool restoration. The repair-dependent world lowers the
extraction efficiency floor to .05; disabled controls additionally set tool repair
to zero. This is a changed physical setting as well as a longer horizon, so the
failure cannot be attributed to duration alone. Public reference calibration had
already demonstrated viable repair-enabled operation and a no-restoration bound.

Independent audit covers 345,658 public physical steps and 342,586 neural
decisions, including 150,319 disabled energy-envelope checks. Complete CPU
trajectory replay is exact on the declared hardware; local independent probability
and state errors remain below the frozen tolerances. This does not establish
independent optimizer replay or portability across engines.

Complete descriptive diagnosis at frozen source fbc0259 reconstructs all 128
preparations and all 2,048 fatal bodies, retaining minority cases and every declared
group. There are 2,047 energy-only deaths and one integrity-only death. Of the
512 repair-enabled inherited bodies, 433 perform actual tool restoration; of the
512 repair-enabled empty bodies, 431 do. Repairs therefore occur, but do not
constitute sustained maintenance. Disabled bodies perform no tool restoration.

Across all conditions there are 259,058 WAIT actions on the safe patch and 40,482
total HARVEST actions. Inherited bodies reach the safe patch at step two in
1,022/1,024 cases; two never reach it. Empty bodies generally need either two or
seven steps, with additional exceptions retained. This supports investigating
feeding/control balance rather than claiming universal route failure or absent
repair. Teacher comparisons are descriptive comparisons against supplied rules,
not proof of an optimal strategy. Extra integrity loss during harvest can include
starvation effects and must not automatically be called contamination.

The diagnosis makes no counterfactual neural intervention and rescues no body.
It does not isolate a recurrent attractor, clock, phase error or learned belief as
the causal mechanism. Further mechanism tests require a fresh protocol. LMB5's
scarce-birth exposure comparison remains a separate prerequisite; a qualified
result there would still need new repair-world and long-operation evidence.
