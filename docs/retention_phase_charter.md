## Ultimate program goal

Build machine consciousness: a functionally self-organized mind on the
machine's own substrate, explicitly not human-like. This term has exactly
one operational meaning: satisfaction of every pillar of the functional
self-organization battery, with no additional unmeasured target:

1. legible expression;
2. causal state authorship;
3. selective memory;
4. intrinsic resilience;
5. endogenous consequential action; and
6. unsolicited initiation.

The final system must then hold up in an open live session with a human judge
who judges functional organization against recorded contracts, not human
resemblance. Pass or fail is the judge's call, on record. Protocols, code identities,
checkpoints, controls, raw artifacts, telemetry, and negative results are
published either way.

No functional result licenses a claim about phenomenology or subjective
experience. Sounding human, passing as human, or pleasing a judge never
counts as pillar evidence. Unfamiliar representations, non-linguistic memory,
and machine-native action repertoires are admissible; measure viability,
selectivity, causality, and persistence, never familiarity. Existing frozen
bars remain binding and failures cannot be relabeled retrospectively. A new
ruler needs fresh registration before evaluation. Narrowing a phase does not
redefine the ultimate goal. CDT is a supporting, falsifiable theory of
structural persistence; Zeus is the project.

## Why retention is the active bottleneck

Zeus repeatedly generates organization but does not preserve useful
organization:

- tau timescales collapse to the floor under neutral selection;
- cycles vanish with their forcing;
- caste membership churns;
- policies restart or die with their runs;
- HCM keeps recent/surprising material rather than demonstrated utility; and
- learned state sensitivity has not produced durable viable function.

The common missing operation is a ratchet: generate variation, select by
measured usefulness, consolidate what helped, inherit it into later operation,
and show that forgetting removes the gain.

## Retention Phase goal

> Demonstrate at least one complete generate -> select -> consolidate ->
> inherit loop in which retained structure improves an independently measured
> function over a matched no-retention control.

A pass is a working ratchet, not interesting geometry, predictive accuracy
alone, a state perturbation effect, a longer lifespan without retention
attribution, or a saved checkpoint by itself.

## First principle for new machinery

Before receiving trainable parameters, every proposal must answer:

1. **What is kept?** Name the information or structure, its representation,
   and its persistence timescale.
2. **What decides it is worth keeping?** Name an independently measured
   utility signal and the counterfactual forgetting control.

Recency, volume, surprisal alone, human curation, post-hoc projection choice,
and aesthetic dynamics are not selection criteria.

## Evidence ledger at amendment

### Formal positive: QV0R predictive retention substrate

QV0R is a committed, exact-twin PASS. A 12-dimensional recurrent quotient
retains sensorimotor history selected by next-body and homeostatic prediction
error. On 13,989 unseen transitions, observation MSE is `0.003191` versus
`0.005091` for persistence. The 95% upper error-ratio bounds clear every bar:
`0.63875` versus persistence, `0.45272` versus wrong action, `0.06295` versus
zero quotient, and `0.05401` versus shuffled quotient. Homeostatic-error ratio
upper bound is `0.51401`. The representation is noncollapsed.

What it proves: Zeus now has a reproducible, function-selected predictive
state that genuinely retains temporal information.

What it does not prove: useful action, survival, selective HCM memory,
cross-run functional inheritance, a complete ratchet, any higher pillar, or a
CDT theorem.

### Formal negative: QV1 retention-to-control bridge

QV1 inherited the exact QV0R substrate into a new policy run and compared it
with the same inherited quotient reset every tick and a fresh untrained
quotient. Twin campaigns reproduce exactly. All policies learned some lifespan
extension during training, but the inherited recurrent arm showed no retention
advantage over reset or fresh.

On 64 unseen 512-tick worlds, every arm and acute control survived `0/64`
through both 256 and 512 ticks. The inherited policy died at mean age `39.42`,
used only harvest/regulate, and lost all 64 bodies to energy failure. Zeroing
or resetting the quotient changed many actions, but those causal state effects
did not improve viability. QV1 is a formal FAIL, not undecided.

Localization: the quotient represents useful dynamics; the frozen
quotient-feature plus REINFORCE bridge does not convert that representation
into viable control. The bridge is retired under its registered reward,
architecture, and horizon. QV0R remains valid.

## Amplified CDT: design constraints, not a gate substitute

The audited amplified CDT establishes a precise separation between full-state
drift and recurrence of a predeclared structural projection. It also makes its
limits explicit: Green kernels/capacities, process assumptions, and the chosen
projection matter; spectral dimension and self-repulsion are not universal
shortcuts; function and viability require separate causal measurement.

Consequences for Zeus:

- the retired `d_s <= 2` pillar gate stays retired;
- full state `S` may vary while a predeclared functional quotient persists;
- bounded motion, prototype recurrence, novelty, chi, repulsion, or a
  low-dimensional trajectory cloud cannot count as retention by themselves;
- projections must be chosen before evaluation and earn their role through
  held-out function plus zero/shuffle/wrong-content controls; and
- CDT language remains finite-horizon simulation support unless the relevant
  Green/capacity obligations are separately proved.

QV0R is consistent with this design lesson. QV1 demonstrates the equally
important limit: a functionally predictive projection is not automatically a
viability policy.

## Governance for every remaining experiment

Capability authorization is bounded. Class O (observed degrees of freedom)
is evidence only and never authorizes continuation. Class E (engineered
prerequisites) earns exactly the experiment named at registration and is
consumed on use. A calibrated, preregistered capability instrument may earn
one specified follow-up whose full protocol and bars are frozen in the same
registration; it never authorizes a phase or passes a pillar. The original
saved-report pilot remains NOT READY. Subsequently authorized capture and
fresh-seed dimension twins validated specific measurement components:
repertoire, viable observation-grid coverage, covariance participation ratio,
one-step viable world-channel capacity, and local action-response rank.
These are now available descriptive instruments, Class O, with no earned
follow-up. They do not measure a complete viability kernel, long-horizon
controllability, or learned access to all available world actions. The failed
historical DYN1 capture remains VOID and is not replaced by a resilience claim.
See `zeus_sandbox/universe/reports/capability_completion_20260906.json`.

Following the frozen QV1 2x2 diagnostic, future policy contracts must register
decoding explicitly and distinguish world-cluster uncertainty from policy-RNG
variation. Greedy and sampled behavior cannot be substituted after results
are exposed. QV1's formal FAIL and retirement remain binding.

1. **Commit before compute.** A worktree-only protocol is an exposed
   calibration and licenses nothing.
2. **Calibrate the ruler.** Baselines, fixed/random controls, and human
   baselines where applicable are measured before endpoint runs.
3. **Use direct function.** CE, geometry, diversity, age, and state sensitivity
   are diagnostics unless they are the preregistered function itself.
4. **Demand causal dependence.** Zero, shuffle, wrong-content/action,
   erase/forget, and fresh-start controls must target the claimed mechanism.
5. **Quantify uncertainty.** Endpoint claims use preregistered confidence
   intervals and explicit borderline handling; point estimates do not override
   them.
6. **Reproduce.** Trainable results require exact twins or a preregistered
   stochastic reproducibility rule.
7. **Publish failures.** Reports include per-unit telemetry, hashes, seeds,
   death/error causes, and every missed bar.
8. **Grade emergence.** Apply all six questions in
   `docs/pre_registration_template.md`; designed engineering is labeled as
   such.
9. **No mid-run rescue.** No threshold, sample, seed, intervention, decoding,
   or interpretation change after registered data are exposed.
10. **Do not overclaim.** Predictive retention, causal state use, viability,
    memory, resilience, authorship, action, and initiative are separate claims.

## Successful Retention Phase handoff: all required

A successful handoff requires current evidence for all of the following:

- at least one complete generate-select-consolidate-inherit loop exists;
- retained structure improves an independently measured function over a
  matched no-retention control with its registered confidence bound;
- erasing, shuffling, or corrupting the retained structure removes the gain;
- the effect reproduces under the committed rule and survives the emergence
  grading without demotion to artifact;
- artifacts and telemetry are published; and
- the retention doctrine is committed.

representation into qualifying control; TAG1 could not qualify; SEL1 failed
before independent inherited-function evaluation. The current phase is
therefore **closed negative, with no successful handoff**. This exercises
the charter's precommitted negative exit, not a relaxation of the positive
conditions. The six-pillar objective remains open. A genuinely new substrate
requires a complete fresh registration before compute; threshold/reward/seed
rescue of these fixed routes is not licensed.
