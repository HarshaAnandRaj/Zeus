# Retention Phase charter: build the ratchet

Status: **ratified 2026-09-05. Governs all work until phase exit. P1 is
conceded for this mouth generation; legibility-gated pillars are out of
scope; no mouth tuning without a genuinely new mechanism.**

## Diagnosis (why this phase exists)

The substrate generates structure constantly (tempo hierarchies, cycles,
castes, policies) and retains none of it: tau decays to floor under neutral
selection, cycles die with their forcing, caste members churn completely,
policies die with their runs, memory keeps the recent instead of the useful.
Every failure in program history is a retention failure wearing a different
mask. Generation is solved and observed; retention exists nowhere — not in
dynamics, not in memory, not across runs.

## Phase goal

> Demonstrate ONE complete retention loop: structure the system generates,
> selects by measured usefulness, consolidates into a persistent substrate,
> and inherits across sessions or runs — with function improved by the
> retained structure versus the no-retention control.

A pass is a working ratchet, not a metaphor. Anything that cannot show
keep-decisions improving function over forgetting fails the phase
regardless of how interesting its dynamics look.

## First principle (gate for all new machinery)

Every proposed mechanism must answer before receiving parameters: **what
does it keep, and what decides what's worth keeping?** Proposals without a
selection criterion are rejected at the whiteboard. Proposals whose
criterion is recency, volume, or human curation are rejected with it.

## Sub-goals (ordered; each pre-registered separately before compute)

1. **QV0 viability quotient** (QV0 exposed calibration unlicensed; QV0R
   completed formal PASS; QV1 inheritance/policy registration licensed). A slow recurrent
   quotient integrating body signals with no sensor shortcut; must predict
   held-out observations beyond persistence with degradation under
   wrong-action and zero/shuffled-quotient controls. Licenses quotient-based
   policy on pass. *Retention reading: the first substrate designed to keep
   rather than merely react.*
2. **Night6 memory-CE replication** (completed FAIL; route closed). Coarse memory
   must beat no-memory on val CE with selective causal audit. *Retention
   reading: tests whether kept structure helps prediction.*
3. **Embodiment policy lineage** (POL3 completed FAIL; quotient lineage is
   contingent on QV0R PASS). Viability bars on unseen
   worlds with state-sensitivity controls. *Retention reading: none yet —
   every run starts from scratch. On pass, the lineage owes a heredity
   design (see 5).*
4. **TAG1 authorship-tagged recall** (pre-registered, unbuilt). Recall must
   carry provenance the dynamics can learn to trust or doubt, beating
   untagged ablation on matched-vs-wrong gain. *Retention reading: selection
   at read time — doubting is keeping's twin.*
5. **Selective retention in HCM** (unbuilt). Writes and eviction gated by
   measured utility, not surprisal and recency; must show positive-utility
   fraction rising across sessions versus the auto-write control.
   *Retention reading: the write-side twin of TAG1's read-side selection.*
6. **Heredity across runs** (unbuilt). New runs initialize from survivor
   substrates (Lamarckian init, as v7a→v8 did for the mouth); must show
   cumulative gain versus fresh-start controls at matched compute.
   *Retention reading: retention across the largest timescale — runs, not steps.*

## Out of scope until exit

Legible expression, causal state expression of speech, unsolicited
initiation, the open human session, mouth tuning of any kind, new drives,
new pillars. Violations require a charter amendment with stated reason —
not enthusiasm.

## Phase exit (all required)

- At least ONE sub-goal 1–6 passes its bars with a demonstrated
  generate→select→consolidate→inherit loop improving function over its
  no-retention control.
- The passing mechanism survives the emergence-grading checklist
  (`docs/pre_registration_template.md`) without demotion to artifact.
- A written retention doctrine replaces this charter: what keeps, what
  decides, and what the next phase (re-engagement of the higher pillars)
  inherits.

If no sub-goal passes after its licensed attempts: the phase ends in a
written negative — retention unachieved with current substrates — and the
program faces the strategic question (new substrate vs narrowed scope)
with a complete map instead of another backlog.
