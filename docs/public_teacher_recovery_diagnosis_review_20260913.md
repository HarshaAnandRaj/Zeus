At32, teacher recovers all reached cases that later die in both arms. This
supports the availability of recoverable public targets early in these histories,
and does not support pervasive teacher incapacity as a complete explanation of
LMB2's learner-history degradation. It does not distinguish truncated credit,
representation aliasing, repeated-write decoder shift or objective interference.
Those remain separate hypotheses. Counterfactual success of a reference policy
does not establish learned recovery, selective memory or a pillar.
