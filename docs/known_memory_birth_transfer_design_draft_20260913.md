## Subsequent inheritance question: do not merge it with transfer

The eventual operating lineage must choose its own actions in the first body,
obtain quality evidence through actual inspection, and carry only declared state
into new bodies. Later sensing stays available and costs real time/energy. Within
each body the world/state continues; cycles do not mean erasing every useful fact.
Boundary resets and inherited components must be explicit and independently verified.

Required controls include intact memory, acute boundary erasure, opposite/permuted
content, disabled writes, and an equally trained forgetting controller. Matched
training must give forgetting its own allowed public acquisition opportunities,
not make it a surprised intact policy. Fatal low-energy histories remain present
with honest inactive padding; no privileged label supplies a fact that it did not
learn. Control source/actuator qualification must prevent interpreting grossly broken
motor control as an information advantage. Shared ecologies/profile/body cycles
are clustered evidence, not multiplied independent samples.

Prespecify native survival/function benefit before exposure. Keep the high-energy
zero-advantage contrasts; do not replace them with inspection counts after a FAIL.
Opposite-content interventions need declared genuine public provenance and matched
encoding nuisance variables, not fabricated observations or an arbitrary latent sign.
The actual learned gradient boundary is also explicit: frozen representation/writes
are prerequisites here, not learned utility selection across lives.

Even a native inheritance PASS leaves selective keeping/forgetting, revision,
reward-based self-directed learning, intrinsic recovery, authorship/priority/
initiative, legible expression and integration owed. It buys the next specified
experiment, not the whole memory pillar or a new definition of the project.
