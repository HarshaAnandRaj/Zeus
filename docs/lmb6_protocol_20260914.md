## Question and engineered affordance

Does a learned correction from current public observations yield a qualified
scarce-birth actuator, and does it improve survival over two declared controls?
These are separate binary judgments. Teacher-specified feeding, repair and
direction are engineered training targets, not discoveries of emergent behavior.
Unprescribed patterns may be recorded independently of usefulness; passing this
campaign does not itself identify an emergent property.

For the ordinary GRU update r, the two new arms compute

    a = tanh(W e + b)
    h = r + (1 - abs(r)) * a
    logits = actor(h + sigmoid(gate([h,z])) * reinstate(z))

Corrected h persists to the next decision. Current uses the eight canonical
public observation features as e; recurrent uses the first eight coordinates of
r. Both add288 parameters and start W=b=0. Legacy has the original architecture
and receives identical additional training. Selection order is current, recurrent,
legacy. The unmodified warm source is a diagnostic baseline. All six actions remain
available to raw softmax sampling; no teacher, action mask or scripted repair
controller runs inside evaluated neural bodies.

Unavailable inspection fields are masked. The current branch includes position
and available inspection features, not just physiology. The recurrent control's
first-eight-coordinate bottleneck is declared, not an information-equivalent or
optimization-equivalent branch. Even a positive architectural comparison does not
isolate physiology or establish optimality against every possible architecture.
For bounded initial h, the slack-scaled correction preserves [-1,1] in real
arithmetic. This does not prove contraction, gradient bounds or useful resilience.

## Architecture attribution and decision

For each energy report paired inherited-survival differences current-minus-
recurrent and current-minus-legacy, plus every fitted arm versus warm. Sample
four parents with replacement and32 ecology quartets with replacement, keeping
all four side/quality conditions in each quartet. Share these10,000 bootstrap
draws across comparisons, and use percentile bounds2.5/97.5%. These are marginal
intervals with only four parent lineages; they are not broad population evidence.

Architecture attribution PASS requires current itself to qualify and all four
scarce comparisons (both controls at both.12 and.20) to have mean>=.02 and lower
bound>0. This is a conjunctive requirement; one favorable contrast is insufficient.
Higher-energy contrasts and warm contrasts are descriptive, not alternative gates.
Any unmet condition yields attribution FAIL while preserving the measured effects.

The functional verdict is PASS if at least one complete arm qualifies; choose the
first in the frozen selection order. Otherwise FAIL. Separately arranged integer
cell gates and paired contrasts must agree with the primary judge. Full physical
and anchored neural replay begins from public sources and computed states;
recorded states are assertions. CPU replay is exact; local independent NumPy
checks require probability error<2e-5 and state error<1e-4. They do not claim
accumulated cross-engine trajectory equivalence. Audit every body and query,
complete fit identities, source hashes, and all final artifacts before qualification.

A qualified arm earns only the already specified native agent-own acquisition and
inheritance experiment, including acute erasure, opposite content, disabled
acquisition writes and matched trained forgetting. A functional FAIL requires
complete diagnosis before another mechanism change. All twelve roadmap problems
and the six-pillar/live-session goal remain open; this campaign cannot close them.
