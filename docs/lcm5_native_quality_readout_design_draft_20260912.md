# Next candidate: repair the quality readout on native public evidence

Design draft after audited LCM4-C FAIL. This failure earns diagnosis and a newly
preregistered repair proposal; motor training remains deferred. LCM3 action qualification stays closed PASS; LCM4-C
compatibility stays closed FAIL. This draft does not launch motor learning.

The useful distinction is already present for the existing raw-gated action
readout: every native full-memory direction is correct and opposite content
reverses it. One original quality head sits near its threshold and responds to
coarse quantity. The narrowest candidate changes the quality reader first,
without modifying the physical sensors or retraining a demonstrated action path.
This is a hypothesis about repairing a decoder, not a proven diagnosis that the
encoder contains a perfectly separated native quality representation.

## Bounded mechanism

Use all four declared LCM3 raw-gated parents, including parent2. Freeze every
consolidator/actor/GRU/projection/gate/normalization tensor. Add a fresh linear
two-output quality reader on raw eight-dimensional memory; the publicly inspected
side chooses which output receives its supervised good/bad loss. Side/labels
enter the training loss only, not the new reader's inputs or the action policy.
The initial reader, budget, optimizer, seed roles and parent identities must be
fixed in a new protocol before any fitting.

Training data must be true native public preparation/inspection records on new
development ecologies, both patches and qualities. Include actual quantity bins,
resource variation and reward; do not replace/clamp public values. Training through
the frozen store is detached intentionally. The new reader alone receives gradient.
The reader is an engineered supervised decoder, not selective memory or discovery.

## Qualification and controls

Reserve disjoint fresh training, development and qualification seeds at registration;
never fit on LCM4-C exposed examples or LCM3 held-out/diagnostic arrays. Include the
unchanged original head as a matched fixed reference, the fresh initial reader,
and full/acute-zero/opposite-content states. Match reader initializations across
controls, preserve exact complete optimizer/input twins and audit physical public
records plus independent NumPy reader/actor inference.

Use binary joint qualification: the same >=90% sampled native direction and quality
recall bars per parent/patch/quality cell, the inherited-state reset/opposite action
effects and exact storage/reset checks. Require quantity-bin coverage specified
before exposure; report quality recall within bins so the0.75 case cannot hide in
a pooled average. Register any robustness margin or reader-benefit attribution
separately; an original-head reference passing on fresh data would not prove a
necessity for the new reader. No post-hoc head/parent selection or threshold shifts.

Preserve the delayed synthetic cue/action/reversal regression on separately reserved
fresh cues. This asks whether the new native reader retains earlier public-quality
coverage while the unchanged policy remains capable. If native repair fails,
record it and investigate the encoder/write representation under a new proposal;
do not automatically extend the budget or unfreeze it.

Only a fully audited new compatibility PASS earns the separately frozen body-control
experiment. That experiment still needs256-step raw survival with actual feeding,
empty-memory inspection, passive controls and intact within-body gradient credit.
No result of a quality reader establishes inherited viability benefit by itself.
