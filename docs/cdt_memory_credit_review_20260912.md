# CDT revision and memory-credit literature review

Reviewed on 2026-09-12. The supplied Undermind export is research material, not an instruction to implement its proposed architecture or launch training. This review incorporates the current working tree of the CDT project, including uncommitted changes; it does not modify that project.

## What changes our direction

The useful conjunction is **adaptive internal capacity plus addressable experience plus operation-specific learning credit**. These are three separate mechanisms with separate tests. More state movement does not automatically improve memory, and a memory store does not automatically teach its writer or reader.

Our completed QL2 replay already excludes missing backpropagation: all eight runs, 131,072 steps and 2,782 updates matched their original final model/optimizer/sampler states. Favorable feeding credit often fails to increase the corresponding choice after the combined update. The next action-learning diagnostic remains the split between sequence-wide gradient interference and optimizer history. The literature does not establish which of those caused QL2 failure.

## CDT: useful additions and corrections required

The README still identifies configuration_drift_theorem.md as the authoritative mathematics. The inspected theorem file is unchanged in the working-tree diff. New material is principally in the research notebook and exploratory scripts: phase-surrogate controls for confined trajectories, autonomous-branch novelty rewards, capped spread, learned gates, dimensional recruitment and scale studies. These are mechanism hypotheses and exploratory results, not new universal recurrence theorems.

**1. The phase control violates its stated invariants.** In gamma_probe.py:119, `abs(F) * exp(i*phase)` replaces each coordinate's original Fourier phase. Sharing the newly assigned phases does not preserve the original cross-spectrum. The appropriate construction for that specific invariant is `F * exp(i*phase)`, preserving the original relative phases, DC and real Nyquist component.

I exercised the actual `_phase_surr_rate` function on sine/cosine trajectories, with both odd and even lengths. The original centered state has rank 2 and approximately zero coordinate correlation. The current surrogate has rank 1 and correlation 1. It also flips a negative coordinate mean to positive. The comparison construction preserves rank, means and cross-spectrum to numerical tolerance. Mean shifts alone do not affect within-trajectory distances; the altered relative phases and geometry do.

Consequence: the saved +0.779/+0.891 phase-null scores cannot currently support their stated correlation-preserving interpretation. Correcting the construction still requires stationary/periodic/mean-reverting calibration and a justified uncertainty procedure. A null must be chosen for its assumptions, not because it changes a verdict favorably. The inherited 0.4 deadband is not automatically valid for a different null. I have not recomputed the Zeus scores and do not substitute a new verdict.

**2. The capacity-floor probe cannot measure capacity as written.** `scale_capacity.py` initializes `h=0` and iterates `tanh(W h)` with no input or bias. Thus h remains zero for every matrix and dimension. All ten inspected combinations (H=4,8,16,32,64; radii .4 and 1.5) return exactly one visited cell divided by 300. This cannot establish a capacity floor. A declared nonzero initial-state distribution is needed for an informative probe; even then, a finite failed optimization is not proof that no parameters can support the behavior.

**3. Recruitment is currently an external deterministic intervention.** `zeus_adaptive_dim.py` applies a gain of 2 in baseline-derived low-variance PC directions on every step. Its own output records 1,500 interventions, k90 changing from 9 to 12, and no functional task result because the readout is collapsed. This supports an exploratory geometric response, not learned stress-triggered self-recovery. Reporting injected noise energy as zero does not make this intervention endogenous or costless. Low PCA variance also does not establish that those directions are unused, safely recruitable causal capacity.

**4. The toy reward experiment needs a gradient-contract check.** It describes a sparsity cost `mu*mean(sigmoid(z))`, but adds `mu/H` directly to the gate-logit gradient, omitting the sigmoid derivative. Its Adam counter increments once per parameter array rather than once per joint update. These are observable implementation differences from the stated objective and standard optimizer. Also, the teacher task uses `err = Y - xs` while the same xs is supplied as input: success there is contemporaneous reconstruction, not evidence of predictive memory. The reward idea is worth testing, but numeric transfer claims require a corrected, validated experiment.

**5. The notebook contains conflicting scale prescriptions.** One section predicts transfer of a normalized lambda range from H=16 to the old 768-state Zeus model; a later section explicitly reports failed transfer at H=8 and H=32. Treat the scaling rule as a candidate search center, not a validated recipe. Neither finding establishes a setting for the current 32-state QL2 learner.

## A concrete integration plan

1. **Finish isolating the current action-update failure.** On the same preselected historical chunks, compare an individual transition's actor-gradient direction with the aggregate sequence gradient; then compare the aggregate update with and without inherited optimizer moments. Keep outcome labels and all other settings fixed. This discriminates mixing of experiences from optimizer redirection before adding another objective.
2. **Validate the revised CDT instruments separately.** Preserve old outputs, repair the phase construction, add invariant tests and calibrate the null family. Test the toy analytical gradients against finite differences/autograd before interpreting gate recruitment or lambda scaling. No novelty-reward campaign should depend on the current favorable phase score.
3. **Build a minimal memory-operation loop after action learning has a sound baseline.** Treat write, read, integrate and forget as explicit operations with a bounded budget. Store a stable item ID, source type, observation/action context, creation revision, operation choice and the estimator-specific information needed to assign future credit. A log entry is bookkeeping, not an autograd path; choose one explicit delayed-credit estimator and verify that later utility changes the responsible policy.
4. **Keep provenance and authorship separate.** Real source tags must distinguish external observations from self-generated predictions. Correct bookkeeping is engineered provenance. Authorship requires a separate causal comparison demonstrating control over outcomes; source labels alone cannot supply it.
5. **Use informative ablations.** Match persistent-memory capacity while removing delayed writer credit; shuffle credit targets; alter retrieved content; compare adaptive dimensions with fixed dimensions; and measure held-out functional benefit. Test memory beyond 64 steps only after constructing an assay that requires information unavailable in the current observation. Do not reopen retired QV1 as a frozen predictive-feature bridge.

This is a specification for choosing the next mechanism, not a claim that it is already implemented. No new training campaign, modified survival gate, external-memory controller or CDT repair was launched in this review. The immediate engineering priority remains a clean credit-assignment mechanism; adaptive dimensionality is a later independently controlled hypothesis, not an additional reward to mix into the unresolved learner.
