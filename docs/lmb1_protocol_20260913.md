## Mechanism and causal/gradient boundary

All four declared LCM5 inherited-reader parents, no model selection. Copy their
raw-gated fast GRU/projection/gate/actor into a body-session module. Those four
modules train. Original consolidator and new quality reader remain frozen exactly.
Fast state, executed previous action/reward and boundary/start fields continue
within the life. Slow state receives actual eligible public inspection transitions.
No per-tick hidden reset, raw-cue bypass or teacher map enters the actor.

Training gradient reaches recurrent fast weights, projection, gate and actor through
body losses, with32-step BPTT truncation that detaches rather than zeroes fast state.
The writer/quality reader are intentionally frozen prerequisites. Public rewards
are previous-action context; this supervised motor stage does not claim learned
reward-based write credit across bodies. Evaluation is fixed-weight; recurrent
updates come from the model's executed actions and actual subsequent observations.

## Public teacher and development calibration

Teacher receives public readings and its own remembered public inspections only.
If no inherited reading, move to left patch and inspect. Otherwise infer safe side
from the public packet and declared one-safe-patch world rule. Navigate to it,
HARVEST below energy.5, otherwise WAIT. Estimate tool from its last public inspection
and declared per-action wear/repair constants; when estimated tool<.75 or public
integrity<.8, navigate to the workshop and MAINTAIN. No seed, hidden safe patch,
quality snapshot, future event or private planner input is accessed. This explicit
teacher is engineered; matching it is not a discovery result.

Before fitting, separate calibration ecologies209023000–209023127, both inspected
patches, known/empty assignment below. Teacher must survive all256-step bodies with
>=16 positive feeding actions and>=1 actual workshop maintenance each. Matched
WAIT bodies must all terminate. Failure is VOID before training; preserve artifacts
and do not alter teacher/world/bars. Calibrate true public physics, not a simulator
surrogate. Audit tool measurements are observer-only and cannot enter teacher/actor.
