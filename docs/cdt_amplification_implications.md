## What was genuinely amplified

1. **The theorem is now precise.** CDT persistence means finite expected
   returns to a registered fine target in full state while a predeclared
   structural projection returns persistently. It is relative to
   `(X, pi, x, epsilon, R)`, not a synonym for life or success.
2. **Green kernels and capacities replace the old slogan.** Spectral dimension
   is only a corollary when two-sided heat-kernel and irreducibility assumptions
   are established. A trajectory-cloud correlation dimension cannot be
   substituted for substrate volume dimension.
3. **High dimension and positive self-repulsion are not necessary.** The
   cleanest registered causal example is a two-dimensional walk whose drift is
   hidden from a one-dimensional projection: full anchored returns decay while
   the projected slope remains recurrent. Put the same drift inside the
   projection and both recur less.
4. **Self-repulsion became broader but not theorem-strength.** The tested
   destination-local-time walk preserves projected finite-horizon recurrence
   through `gamma=10`, while full historical recurrence falls. Sparse full
   anchored returns prevent an asymptotic transience claim.
5. **Failure modes are explicit.** Anchored, historical, and projected
   recurrence cannot be interchanged; constant or post-hoc projections are
   vacuous; finite irreducible spaces require capacity/cover-time analysis;
   and functional benefit needs an independent causal task metric.
6. **Viability has its own mathematics.** Sustained operation depends on the
   viability kernel and admissible sensing/action/energy constraints. It cannot
   be reduced to a scalar combination of `d_s` and `gamma`.

## New design obligation

Zeus currently asks one drifting 768-dimensional `S` to carry exploration,
memory address, body variables, and policy control. POL2 shows that an action
head can read this state causally; POL3 shows that changing reward and horizon
does not make the relevant homeostatic structure persist. The amplified CDT
suggests a testable replacement:

1. Introduce a small learned viability quotient `V`, updated only from bodily
   observations, previous `V`, selected action, and observed action outcome.
2. Predeclare `pi(S,V)=V` and train `V` to predict next bodily state and
   homeostatic error on held-out transitions. This makes the projection
   functionally testable rather than geometrically convenient.
3. Let the full `S` continue to vary; do not penalize it into a fixed point.
   Policy reads `V` (and optionally a causally ablated `S` branch), never raw
   observations.
4. Require independent evidence: held-out viability, calibrated controls,
   `V`-shuffle/zero interventions, `S`-only and `V`-only ablations, exact twin
   replay, and a structurally different world.
5. CDT language remains finite-horizon simulation support unless full Green or
   capacity obligations and projected recurrence are separately established.

This quotient mechanism can serve more than embodiment if it works: selective
memory should key on a learned outcome-predictive quotient rather than random
state projection, and a future mouth bridge should translate an independently
validated intent quotient rather than infer authorship from arbitrary `S`
motion. Those are later experiments, not consequences of the theory alone.
