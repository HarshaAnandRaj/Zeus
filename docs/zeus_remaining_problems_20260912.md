# Zeus: the problems remaining beyond a usable memory path

Roadmap created 2026-09-12; evidence refreshed 2026-09-13. Planning only: this list does not launch experiments,
change frozen bars, reopen a closed verdict or establish a pillar result. The user
asked for a durable list so that local repairs do not displace the program goal.

The destination remains the charter's machine-specific functional organization:
legible expression, causal state authorship, selective memory, intrinsic resilience,
endogenous consequential action and unsolicited initiation, followed by the declared
live human session. No functional finding establishes subjective experience.
Zeus is the project; CDT and adaptive dimensionality are supporting hypotheses.

## Where we stand

LCM5's fresh native quality reader has an independently audited PASS on fixed
stores and actors, including delayed recall controls. This resolves the named
decoder blocker in its declared setup. LCM4-C's original quality-head compatibility
FAIL remains closed. LMB1's complete training and endpoint show substantial motor
learning but miss four 90% survival cells; a frozen JSON reporting defect makes
the campaign VOID. Its postmortem preserves the underlying functional FAIL.
LMB2's learner-history correction fails, while its demonstration control meets
the raw functional gates. However, the independent accumulating NumPy replay
exceeds its frozen probability tolerance, so LMB2 is VOID and neither arm is
qualified for transfer. A fresh, prospectively validated replay instrument and
fresh controller qualification are required; the old tolerance cannot be changed
to rescue the exposed campaign.

An independently audited reference-controller calibration shows that inherited
information can improve survival in the declared scarce-resource birth family.
This is evidence that the world can measure the intended contrast, not that the
neural agent has demonstrated it. LBT1 is prepared but remains unlaunched because
its audited LMB2 prerequisite was not earned.

Storage identity and supplied inspection are engineered. Current evidence does
not establish learned write selection, sustained body control, native inherited
viability benefit, self-directed learning or any of the six complete pillars.
Resolving the reader earned motor preparation, not closure of every memory
question. In particular, an agent may survive after forgetting by inspecting again;
body-control success alone cannot establish an inheritance benefit.

## Remaining problems and evidence we would need

The first two are the immediate functional sequence after compatibility. The later
items are research obligations; their order will depend on what qualified results
and discoveries reveal. Each new experiment still needs its own frozen protocol.

|---|---|---|---|

Fresh instrument validation and qualification come first. Longer operation where repair is necessary remains separately owed. |

| 5 | Revision without destructive interference | During continuing operation, new evidence corrects stale beliefs while preserving still-useful knowledge; stable and changing worlds distinguish appropriate revision from indiscriminate change. | Static one-safe-patch inheritance is insufficient. Multiple facts, contradictory evidence and repeated changes need their own task and controls. |
| 6 | Causal authorship of internal changes and outputs | Identify model-selected actions/writes and trace how those choices causally shape subsequent states and useful outputs under matched interventions. | Dependence on initial state, causal action flips and an externally supplied cue do not by themselves establish authorship. | 7 | Learning about its own capabilities | Experience produces useful predictions about the consequences and limitations of its own actions, including changes in tool or actuator effectiveness. | Public energy and a supplied reward are not a learned self-model. | 8 | Intrinsic resilience | The system detects and recovers from declared disturbances through its own permitted activity, preserving useful organization and outperforming matched recovery controls. | Bounded activations and protected writes are engineered stability. CDT-inspired drift or adaptive capacity needs independent functional benefit; external perturbation must not do the recovery for it. |
| 9 | Endogenous consequential action | Internal conditions organize useful action priorities across competing needs, with causal tests of those internal conditions and real world consequences. | Our viability objective is designer-supplied. Existing actions are consequential, but the full endogenous-action pillar remains separately owed. |
| 10 | Unsolicited initiation | It initiates a useful investigation, repair or other declared activity when appropriate, without a prompt or an external scheduler choosing that activity. | Merely running a policy every tick is not an initiation result. Timing, trigger provenance and functional consequence require explicit controls. |
| 11 | Legible expression owned by the functioning system | A new expression route communicates intelligible content grounded in the operative internal state, with raw free-run and fixed-history causal attribution. | The old mouth/P1 is frozen and failed. A state-dependent lab readout whose state is zeroed at use does not establish expression by the operating system. Reopening requires a new mechanism and the charter's calibrated ruler. |
| 12 | Integrated, persistent organization | Qualified memory, revision, maintenance, action and expression coexist in long operation across declared unseen conditions, followed by the six-pillar battery and live session. | Separate component passes can fail when combined. ## Obligations throughout the sequence

**Trace the whole causal and learning chain.** Public observation → model action → real
world consequence → next public observation → state update/consolidation → later read → later
action must be explicit. During training, verify gradients reach the intended
trainable modules and that parameter changes affect subsequent behavior. A frozen
encoder can be a declared prerequisite; an unnoticed detached learning path cannot.
During evaluation, distinguish recurrent adaptation from external optimizer updates.

**Keep discovery open.** Maintain the observation ledger for small rhythms,
concentrated motion, hidden-state distinctions, cancellation, persistence and other
unexpected patterns. Admission does not require utility. Diagnose mechanism and
function afterward; low covariance dimension or recurrence is not automatically
benefit, authorship or life. A discovery may motivate a new experiment without
becoming a target we force the model to reproduce.

**Preserve scientific continuity.** Commit before compute, keep fresh role-separated
data, exact twins, public provenance, independent physical/neural audits, frozen
PASS/FAIL/VOID rules and confidence bounds. Publish negative contrasts and retain
closed failures. Each component earns only its explicitly registered follow-up.
