# OL4-C4: prospective held-out acquisition protocol

**Status:** Prospective, 2026-09-24. Commit this protocol and its evaluator-only implementation before creating the C4 identity archive. Commit and hash-verify that archive before loading any trained program against it. Do not evaluate a candidate, inspect its C4 performance, or change this protocol while either freeze is incomplete.

## Question and provenance

C4 asks whether the **four already trained OL4-T0a-R1 full programs** acquire fresh marker, mode, lexical, and actuator-rule information during new lifetimes drawn from the existing toy task family. The four separately trained no-write programs remain matched controls. The [development protocol](ol4_t0a_development_protocol.md) and [development result](ol4_t0a_development/final_result.json) reported a development `PASS`; they are not held-out evidence. C4's additional complete-life and correction-cell criteria below were chosen **after seeing that development result but before creating or scoring any C4 identity**. Report that provenance with the result.

No outer training, checkpoint selection, hyperparameter adjustment, source repair, or new optimizer seed belongs to C4. C4 evaluates the frozen eight step-2,000 checkpoints only. A later architecture or generator revision requires a new named experiment and new identities; it cannot turn a C4 failure into a pass.

## Independent held-out identities

Generate **one** CPU archive of `N = 8,192` complete lives in increasing life-index order with namespace `OL4-C4/heldout/identities/v1`. Each named stream uses a fresh NumPy `PCG64` seeded by the little-endian integer in the first eight bytes of `SHA256(namespace + "/" + stream_name)`. The C4 generator must be evaluator-only and must not import the development archive, reuse its RNG states, or change the frozen task grammar. It may construct the same `EvaluatorBatch`, `LifeSchedule`, `PublicTeachingBatch`, and `ActionUniformBatch` schema. An independent validator must check the resulting event and factor constraints rather than trusting generator success.

The registered stream names are `context_extra`, `context_order`, `token_extra`, `token_order`, `mode_swap`, `rule_on`, `safe_left_0`, `safe_left_1`, `word_on_0`, `word_on_1`, `query_first`, `correction_pattern_slot`, `primary_extra`, `primary_order`, `exposure_order`, `initial_demo_before_0`, `initial_demo_before_1`, `correction_demo_before_0`, `correction_demo_before_1`, `marker_schedule`, `decoy_channels`, `decoy_sides`, `move_0..2`, `press_0..2`, and `delay_0..2_extra` plus `delay_0..2_order` (expand each range to the three decimal names). Each stream advances only for its named field. For `correction_pattern_slot`, each 256-life block starts with the sixteen integers `0..15` repeated sixteen times and receives one fresh within-block permutation from that stream; the high bits encode the three flip flags and the low bit the corrected binding slot. For `primary_extra`, take the first two entries of a permutation of `0,1,2`; each gets one extra life, then `primary_order` permutes all 8,192 positions. For ordered context/token pairs and each query delay, use quotient/remainder balancing with the corresponding `*_extra` and `*_order` streams, as in the committed development generator, with the new count and namespace. All other balanced binary factors are a permutation of equal False/True counts from their named streams.

Keep the existing support: two distinct target channels from the same eight one-hot context glyphs; two distinct token rows from the same four; two independently varying safe sides and word meanings; one global KEEP/SWAP and one global actuator rule; two demonstrations of each rule phase; three queries; the registered correction and 4–12 distractors per interval; 12 competing marker-form decoys; 14 bank records; explicit between-query world reset. Preserve the existing action sampler and sampled MOVE followed by conditional PRESS. Do not add an unseen channel, operator, grammar, task, or sensory modality to this in-distribution C4.

Balance the archive prospectively. The 56 ordered distinct context pairs each occur 146 or 147 times (`16` pairs have the extra life); the 12 ordered distinct token pairs each occur 682 or 683 times (`8` have the extra life). Each binary global and per-binding factor occurs 4,096/4,096. Each query delay in 4–12 occurs 910 or 911 times. Within every consecutive 256-life block, place exactly 16 lives in each of the 16 publicly inferable `(correction flip pattern, corrected binding slot)` cells, then independently permute their order; this gives 1,024 lives per flip pattern and 4,096 per corrected binding slot overall. Public teaching must be the truthful packet for each evaluator life before any control shuffle.

Freeze six independent float32 uniforms in `[0,1)` per life, one MOVE and one conditional PRESS uniform at each of three queries. Freeze a separate, evaluator-private primary-query selector before model loading: two of indices `0,1,2` occur 2,731 times and the remaining index occurs 2,730 times, according to `primary_extra` and `primary_order`. The selector is never a learner input. Save all evaluator factors, complete schedules, public teaching, uniforms, selector, named seeds, field dtypes/shapes/hashes, factor counts, source hashes, and archive SHA-256 in an exclusive NPZ plus JSON manifest. Reopen and validate every field against the committed manifest; no archive regeneration, discard, or replacement after creation. Exact factor combinations may recur in a finite grammar; do not filter them post hoc. The independent RNG namespace and untouched archive, rather than a claim of unseen factor values, define the holdout.

Before the first trained-policy evaluation, run an independent scoring oracle on fixed synthetic cases crossing all 16 combinations of the queried binding's safe side and word meaning with global mode and actuator rule, both possible first-query slots, all eight correction flip patterns, and all four legal joint MOVE/PRESS choices. It must use evaluator-private factors and sampled action identities, without calling the production reward calculation. During C4 adjudication, independently reconstruct all three binary rewards for every saved evaluation life and variant from frozen factors, action traces, correction status, and task reset, and require exact agreement. The oracle receives no authority to change an outcome.

## Evaluation and interventions

Use the same frozen identity archive, life order, public schedules, and six uniforms for all four runs and every variant. Evaluate with `torch.no_grad()` in sequential 256-life slices; record device and exact Torch/CUDA versions. Evaluation makes no optimizer step and never carries learner or world state across lives. The full program for each run receives six evaluations: unlesioned, four acute owner-write lesions (`marker`, `mode`, `lexical`, `rule`), and shuffled public teaching. Its matched no-write program receives one unshuffled evaluation. Thus there are **28** immutable evaluation units. A lesion uses the full program's identical parameters and suppresses only that owner's writes at every route, including agent-generated relational transitions. Other owners, decay clocks, bank occupancy, event timing, scoring truth, and exogenous uniforms remain paired. Later policies and public agent outcomes may legitimately diverge after different sampled actions.

For the shuffled control, call the frozen `shuffle_public_teaching` implementation on the C4 archive with seed `7301`. It independently deranges complete public packets for the four owners within each 256-life block and public `(correction pattern, corrected binding slot)` cell; the frozen implementation derives owner seeds as `7301 + 104729 * (owner_index + 1)` in `marker, mode, lexical, rule` order. Archive every donor-index array and stratum key. Require exact within-stratum derangement and packet-multiset preservation, zero singleton strata, and strictly more than `0.20` of complete packets actually changed for **each** owner. Private scoring factors, recipient context/token identities, event envelopes/timing, and action uniforms stay fixed. The control is deliberately corrupted public teaching; it does not describe a naturally occurring distribution shift.

Every unit saves an exclusive raw NPZ and summary JSON with source/model/identity hashes, variant, write permissions, public teaching hash, 8,192×3 sampled rewards and action indices, primary rewards, event counts, bank counts, and reset counts. Require 14 bank records and reset counts `0,1,2` for each life; require finite policy quantities, legal actions, exact summary-to-raw agreement, and oracle-to-raw reward agreement. Preserve all failed and partial artifacts. No evaluation is rerun for a better random draw; an interrupted unit may resume only by byte-verifying an already complete immutable result or recomputing the same missing unit from the same checkpoint, archive, and uniforms into a new exclusive path.

## Endpoints and exact statistics

The **primary** binary outcome for a life is the sampled correct joint MOVE+PRESS at its preassigned query. The **complete-life co-primary** is one iff all three sampled queries succeed in that life. Both have one Bernoulli observation per life. Query-position rates, the query-3 rate in each of the eight correction patterns, three-query mean, and the 28 unordered-context-pair three-query means are registered guards or diagnostics as specified below. Chance for a single joint query is `0.25`; a complete-life chance value is not assumed because action histories and outcomes are dependent.

For a binary arm or cell with `s` successes among `n` lives, use a two-sided 99% Wilson interval, `z = 2.5758293035489004`, without continuity correction. Factor balancing creates heterogeneous success probabilities, so Wilson is a fixed decision statistic, not an exact IID coverage claim. Use strict comparisons. For each run and owner, compute `full_primary[i] - acute_lesion_primary[i]` on paired life `i`. Generate 10,000 paired bootstrap replicates, resampling life indices within each of the 28 unordered context-pair strata with replacement at the original stratum size and reweighting by original stratum size / 8,192. Use `PCG64` from low-64-bit SHA-256 seed of `OL4-C4/bootstrap/paired/v1/{run_id}/{owner}`; NumPy `quantile(..., 0.005, method="linear")` is the 99% lower decision bound. Preserve the full seed and stratum counts.

The **architecture replicate** is an independently trained run, not one of its lives. Report all four run-level point estimates and intervals. For a crossed hierarchical decision statistic, generate 20,000 replicates separately for each named metric from `PCG64` low-64-bit SHA-256 of `OL4-C4/bootstrap/crossed/v1/{metric}`. In each replicate, resample four run IDs with replacement; independently resample original-size life indices within each of the 28 unordered context-pair strata, using the **same sampled life indices for every selected run and every paired variant**. Compute each selected run's weighted metric and average the four selected runs. Use NumPy's `linear` 0.005 quantile for lower bounds and 0.995 quantile for upper bounds. Metrics are `full_primary`, `full_all_three`, `lesion_{owner}_primary_difference` for each owner, `shuffled_primary`, and `no_write_primary`. This preserves common-life and within-run pairing; with only four outer runs, its tail estimate is a registered decision rule with limited seed-population precision. Do not pool 32,768 life outcomes and call them independent architecture replicates.

## C4 adjudication

All 28 evaluation units and all integrity checks above must be valid before a functional decision. `PASS` requires **every** strict condition below:

1. For **each of four runs**, the full program's primary and all-three-success 99% Wilson lower bounds both exceed `0.80`. Each of its three separate query-position Wilson lower bounds exceeds `0.80`. At query three, each of the eight correction-pattern cell Wilson lower bounds exceeds `0.80`. Each of the 28 unordered context-pair means of all three query rewards exceeds `0.75`.
2. For **each run and each of four owners**, the paired acute-lesion bootstrap 99% lower bound on full-minus-lesion primary success exceeds `0.15`.
3. For **each run**, the shuffled-teaching and separately trained no-write primary Wilson 99% upper bounds are each below `0.30`.
4. Across the four run-level estimates, the crossed-bootstrap 0.005 bounds on `full_primary` and `full_all_three` exceed `0.80`, and those on each of four lesion differences exceed `0.15`. Its 0.995 bounds on `shuffled_primary` and `no_write_primary` are below `0.30`.

`FAIL` means complete, valid evidence misses any strict functional threshold, including equality. `VOID` means the archive, protocol/source/checkpoint hashes, frozen factors, public/private boundary, scorer oracle, permutation/control audit, lesions, raw unit completeness, resources, or numeric validity fails; do not convert a VOID into a scientific failure or choose a replacement archive under this version. With binary outcomes, deterministic bounds, and strict inequalities, no `UNDECIDED` branch is planned; an unresolved integrity or numeric computation is `VOID`. Publish exact gate outcomes, per-run metrics, bootstrap seeds/bounds, all raw unit hashes, and a critical review even if the aggregate verdict fails. A new experiment after `FAIL` or `VOID` requires a versioned diagnosis and fresh identity material, not a post-hoc threshold or scalar rescue.

## Claim boundary

A C4 `PASS` supports fresh-life acquisition and correction by these **four frozen inherited programs** within the existing eight-channel, four-token, fixed-XOR/equality, three-query toy family. The paired lesions support reliance on each write route under this evaluation; the separately trained no-write control addresses whether lifetime writes improve attained performance under the matched development training budget. The operator skeleton, ownership graph, event segmentation, token equality addressing, task schedule, and action planner are engineered. C4 does **not** show learning a new compartment topology, a ninth context channel, a fifth token row, unseen operators, unconstrained language, structural rewiring, metaplasticity, criticality, recursive self-improvement, or an advantage over a matched shared core. With four training seeds, even four successes give a 99% one-sided exact binomial lower bound of only about `0.316` on the success fraction of hypothetical independent retrainings; broad seed reliability remains unproved. C5 and C6 require separate prospective contracts.
