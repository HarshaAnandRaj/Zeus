# OL4-T0a development protocol

**Status:** Prospective. Freeze this file, the T0a implementation, and the 4,096-life identity archive before opening any outer optimization seed. The [T0 contract](../docs/v4_outer_training_readiness.md) and [T0a amendment](../docs/v4_t0a_amendment.md) govern; this file fixes operational choices that those documents leave open. This is a development readiness assay, not a held-out architecture result.

## Gate order and stopping

The source-hashed T0a preflight must pass boundary, explicit reset, source routes and lesions, coordinate support and quotient rank, complete-life objective estimator, resource manifest, and shuffle implementation in that order. A failed gate receives an immutable `FAIL` artifact and stops before optimization. An integrity exception before adjudication receives `VOID`. No outer seed opens unless preflight is `PASS`, this protocol is committed, and the development identity archive and manifest are created exclusively and hash-verified. No threshold, schedule, control, or seed change is allowed after that point under this version.

## Fixed identities and random streams

The 4,096 development lives are generated once by `ol4_development.py` with namespace `OL4-T0a/development/identities/v1`: SHA256 of `namespace/name`, low 64 bits little endian, initializes NumPy PCG64 for each named factor, schedule component, action uniform, and preassigned scored-query selector. Save them exclusively to `organized_learner/evidence/ol4_t0a_development_identities.npz` and save the returned manifest, including the archive SHA256, exclusively to `organized_learner/evidence/ol4_t0a_development_manifest.json`. The archive contains 30 fields, including evaluator-private truth, all public-event scheduling and teaching packets, six float32 action uniforms per life, and one selector in `{0,1,2}` per life. The selector never enters the learner or optimizer. Ordered context pairs occur 73 or 74 times, ordered token pairs 341 or 342, binary global and binding-slot factors are 2,048/2,048, correction patterns occur 512 each, and scored-query counts are 1,365/1,365/1,366. The manifest records every field hash, shape, dtype, seed, and balance count. Every evaluation reloads the same archive with the frozen expected manifest and archive SHA256; regeneration cannot replace it. Pre-optimization audit inspects the exact identities and shuffle packet structure. The prohibition on opening development lives before step 2,000 means no trained-policy performance scoring, selection, or stopping on them before then; it does not forbid generation and structural audit.

The four independent outer run IDs are `4101`, `4102`, `4103`, and `4104`. For each run, initialize the 87-scalar `InheritedProgram` with that integer seed using its committed CPU `torch.Generator`. For the three training streams `factors`, `schedule`, and `actions`, derive a separate 63-bit Torch seed as low 64 SHA256 bits of `OL4-T0a/outer/v1/{run_id}/{stream}`, masked to `2^63-1`. The full and no-write arms of a run use the same initial seed and the same three stream seeds. Streams advance in step order and are never reset within a run. Training uses the committed Torch and CUDA versions, float32, on `cuda:0` if available at launch, otherwise CPU; archive the chosen device, versions, source hashes, and seed derivations. The development archive always originates on CPU and is copied to the training device only in fixed sequential evaluation slices of 256 lives; no development data enters a training step.

For the shuffled-source evaluation, use `shuffle_public_teaching` with seed `5703` on the frozen 4,096 identities. The stratum key is `floor(life_index/256)*16 + public_correction_pattern*2 + correction_binding_slot`, where the pattern is computed from the initial and corrected **public** teaching packets. Four owner streams use independent Torch seeds `5703 + 104729*(owner_index+1)` in the committed source order `marker, mode, lexical, rule`. Each stream deranges complete owner packets only within its stratum. The donor-index array for each owner, stratum key, packet multiset identity, singleton count, and actually changed fraction are audited and hashed before evaluation. The structural shuffle gate requires zero singleton strata, every donor array to be a within-stratum derangement/permutation, and **more than 0.20 of complete packets actually changed for each owner**. Evaluator truth, recipient context/token identities, event timing, and action uniforms remain fixed. This is a deliberately corrupted public teaching control.

## Training and evaluation

Train **eight** complete programs: full and all-write-disabled controls for each of the four run IDs. Each uses exactly 2,000 Adam steps, learning rate `0.003`, no weight decay, batch size 512 complete fresh lives per step, gradient norm cap 1.0, and no learning-rate schedule or early stopping. Generate one factor batch, one life schedule, and six action uniforms from their named training streams at each step. `faithful_teaching_batch` constructs public teaching. The full arm enables all four owner writes. The no-write arm disables marker, mode, lexical, and relational writes at all initial, agent-result, and correction routes while retaining all 87 allocated parameters, clocks, bank occupancy, event/action paths, optimizer, objective, and budget. No policy or world state is carried across lives. The loss is the committed `reinforce_loss(training_signals(run_life(...)), entropy_coefficient=0.01)`, with all three sampled rewards and the exact future-entropy trajectory term. The optimization objective is the mean of three query rewards plus `0.01` times the mean of their entropies. No auxiliary targets, teacher-forced actions, truncated graph, interim development evaluation, or adaptive stopping are allowed.

Only after step 2,000 is complete, evaluate each final program with `torch.no_grad()` and the frozen development archive, in life-index order. The full program gets six evaluations on the same factors, schedule, and uniforms: unlesioned, one acute write lesion for each of the four owners, and the shuffled public teaching control. The no-write program gets one unshuffled evaluation. Acute lesions change only named write permission; the program weights, evaluator truth, public schedule, and uniforms are paired. Evaluation retains all three query rewards, actions, event and bank counts, and the preassigned primary-query reward per life. The shuffled-source arm is evaluated with the **trained full** program. No-write is a separately trained control, never a post-training lesion.

## Registered adjudication

The primary binary endpoint is the sampled correct joint MOVE+PRESS outcome at the **one preassigned scored query per life**. A query is successful only if both selected actions are correct in the current public world. This yields 4,096 binary primary outcomes per arm. Three individual query success rates, their per-life mean, and all-three-success are diagnostics; they cannot replace the primary endpoint. Training still uses all three rewards. The 28 unordered context-pair floor is a secondary point criterion computed from the mean of all three query rewards for lives in each pair.

Compute each primary success 99% two-sided Wilson interval with `z = 2.5758293035489004`, `n = 4096`, and no continuity correction. The Wilson bound is a registered decision statistic on this fixed factor-balanced population; the outcomes need not be identical Bernoulli trials. Use strict inequalities throughout. A full run qualifies only if its Wilson lower bound exceeds `0.80`, the paired full-minus-acute-lesion 99% lower bound exceeds `0.15` for **each** of four owners, and each unordered context pair's three-query mean success exceeds `0.75`. At least three of four full runs must qualify. In **each** of four runs, the shuffled-source Wilson upper bound and independently trained no-write Wilson upper bound must each be below `0.30`.

For each acute lesion and run, compute per-life differences between the full and lesioned primary binary outcomes using the identical exogenous identities and action uniforms. Use 10,000 stratified paired bootstrap replicates. The 28 strata are unordered context pairs, with each stratum resampled with replacement at its original size; aggregate using original stratum size/4,096 weights. A separate NumPy PCG64 seed for each run/owner is derived from SHA256 low 64 bits of `OL4-T0a/bootstrap/v1/{run_id}/{owner}`. The 0.005 empirical quantile by NumPy's `linear` method is the conservative two-sided 99% lower bound. Report the point difference, lower bound, and each owner separately; no pooled-life claim substitutes for four independent training runs.

`PASS` requires all registered conditions. `FAIL` applies if all eight training units and evaluations are valid but any strict condition fails. `VOID` applies to a broken identity/hash, private-information boundary, incomplete run, nonfinite optimization, inconsistent event/resource record, altered source, or invalid control. `UNDECIDED` applies only if a valid result meets none of those defects but a declared numerical boundary cannot be resolved at the stored precision; threshold equality itself is a `FAIL` under the strict rule. Preserve exact run statuses, completed steps, parameters/checkpoint hashes, per-life endpoints, intervals, and source hashes. Do not retune this version on its development results.

Even `PASS` establishes only that this engineered organization can be shaped by outer training on this registered toy family and that four sources matter for its learned policy. It does not establish new topology, broad language, critical dynamics, structural rewiring, recursive self-improvement, a held-out generalization claim, or a compartment advantage over a matched shared core.
