# Review 06: OL4-T0 pre-implementation prosecution

**Verdict:** `PASS` as an implementation contract after revision. **Empirical status:** no OL4 code, mechanics result, or training result exists at this checkpoint.

## Why a new version is required

OL3-F1 passed because an engineered four-source solver was correct. Training its few confidence constants would not answer whether phylogenetic-style optimization can shape a useful lifetime learner. OL4-T0 therefore makes retrieval projections, evidence polarity, retention, source gains, and action confidence inherited trainable parameters while preserving a declared compartment graph and operator skeleton.

The first draft was rejected as implementation-incomplete. Independent prosecution identified ten issues:

1. Two marker records did not justify an interference claim.
2. Owner update order and outcome recipients were underspecified.
3. Several bounded-parameter initializations were missing and retention could begin near erasure.
4. Batch-mean nonzero gradients could hide active routes through sign cancellation.
5. A one-query estimator check could miss delayed credit across action-conditioned writes.
6. Exact context permutation equivariance conflicted with learned per-channel projections.
7. Training and development identity separation was not defined.
8. Reward and agent outcomes could create undeclared later-query channels.
9. Shortcut controls lacked executable definitions and thresholds.
10. Random batches did not guarantee coverage of relational indices, context channels, token rows, and correction cases.

## Material revisions

The final contract adds 12 marker-form decoys, giving two target bindings real attention competition while explicitly excluding an eviction claim. It fixes decay-before-write recurrences for every owner, states that reward has no learner-state recipient, makes workspace state ephemeral, and defines acute lesions over every owner write including agent transitions.

All 87 parameter initializations and transforms are explicit. Retentions begin near 0.95, and nonzero evidence/projection initialization breaks the source-product saddle. The gradient gate now uses per-life Jacobian support and finite differences on a deterministic 1,008-life coverage fixture.

The estimator gate now enumerates all 64 action branches of a three-query life, including action-conditioned outcomes and future writes. Context claims are limited to eight inherited glyph channels; token equivariance remains structural, while context performance is stratified across all 28 channel pairs.

The development gate uses four fixed outer seeds, disjoint RNG streams, 4,096 frozen evaluation lives, 99% confidence rules, paired acute lesions, a separately trained no-write control, and a precisely stratified shuffled-source control. Later-query taint checks prohibit reward or workspace carryover from replacing a lesioned owner.

## What the contract can adjudicate

A full T0 pass would show that complete-life outer optimization learned useful evidence polarities, persistence, source scaling, and two-item contextual retrieval inside the supplied owner graph and XOR/equality planner. Failure can be localized to boundary, route, gradient, estimator, reset, resource, shortcut, or optimization gates.

It cannot establish topology discovery, neural population dynamics, criticality, E/I benefit, consolidation, metaplasticity, structural rewiring, operator invention outside the paired grammar, natural language, partition advantage, or recursive improvement. Those remain later architectural questions with their own identifying tasks and controls.

## Decision

Proceed to implementation of the 87-parameter tensor program and public-life generator. Run boundary, route, gradient, full-life estimator, reset, and resource gates before any optimization. Freeze and commit the implementation plus a separate development protocol before opening the four optimization seeds.
